01 · The Idea

Old world ice cream.
New world techniques.

Every batch is built by hand and flash-frozen with liquid nitrogen. Small batch, chef-driven, and seriously dense.

Freezing that fast keeps the ice crystals microscopic, so what you get is creamier than any churn can manage. Frozen to order at your event or scooped from a pint we dropped at your local shop, it's the same Colorado ingredients and the same chef's palate, across Denver, Boulder & the Front Range.

02 · The Science

Why nitro tastes
so much better.

It's simple physics. Liquid nitrogen meets the base at −321°F and freezes it in seconds, long before big, icy crystals ever get a chance to form.

  1. 01

    Pour

    Fresh base meets liquid nitrogen and the temperature drops off a cliff.

  2. 02

    Freeze

    In seconds, only microscopic ice crystals form, the secret to the texture.

  3. 03

    Serve

    The nitrogen fully evaporates. What's left is the creamiest scoop around.
